Visiting this website

Listening needs no account. This website uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Like every website, it runs on a web host that may keep technical logs (such as your IP address and the pages requested) for a short time to keep the server secure and running.

Cookies

This website sets one cookie, named stage, and only after you log in. It keeps you logged in and protects your actions against forged requests. It is removed when you log out or after 30 days.

Login with Google

You can log in with your Google account to like tracks, write comments and follow this website. Google then tells this website your name, your profile picture and your mail address, nothing else. Your Google password never reaches this website. Google's own handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Likes, comments and follows

Your name, profile picture and mail address are stored on this website's server, together with your likes, comments and follows. Comments are shown with your name and picture after the owner approved them, your mail address is never shown. Profile pictures are loaded from Google's servers.

Mails

If you follow this website, you get a welcome mail and a mail whenever a new track is published. Every mail contains a link to unfollow, which stops the mails at once.

Your rights

You can ask which data about you is stored, have it corrected or deleted, and object to its use. Write to the address above. Deleting removes your likes, comments and follow. The data is used on the basis of your consent when you log in or follow (Art. 6 (1) a GDPR) and of the legitimate interest in running this website (Art. 6 (1) f GDPR). You can also complain to a data protection authority.
